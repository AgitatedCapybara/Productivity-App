# Ethical Upsell Copy Guidelines (Keystone Manifesto)

Keystone rejects coercive monetization tactics. We believe power-user features should be adopted based on choice, respect, and mutual alignment, never through anxiety-inducing barriers, simulated scarcity, or pop-up blockouts.

---

## 1. Forbidden Language & Anti-Patterns
To preserve the focus, pride, and sanity of our users, certain manipulative terms are strictly **forbidden** in the codebase, layouts, and public messaging:

| Forbidden Dark Pattern Terms | Reason for Exclusion |
|:---|:---|
| `"limit reached"`, `"quota exceeded"` | Implies user-authored data belongs to the company, causing artificial panic. |
| `"upgrade now"`, `"unlock features"` | Coercive pressure tone; implies the software is artificially holding back functionality. |
| `"premium only"`, `"restricted access"`| Creates a feeling of being an outsider or a second-class user. |
| `"buy today save 50%"` | Manipulative urgency that disrupts work-state mindfulness. |

---

## 2. Preferred Tone & Visual Formatting
When proposing Pro capabilities, speak clearly, objectively, and with supportive composure. Explain the added value of the power features, and always let the user dismiss or proceed smoothly.

### Preferred Copy Templates:
- `"Keystone Pro adds assisted scheduling..."`
- `"When you're ready, Pro extends your analytics with long-term correlations..."`
- `"This feature is part of our professional suite. You can try it free, or continue using our robust offline free edition."`

### Design Pillars for Pro Enhancements:
- **No Nag Screens**: Upsell details remain encapsulated in dedicated settings regions or subtle inline boxes that match the ambient space.
- **Immediate Bypass Availability**: Every feature introduction card MUST include a `"Continue with free version"` option that renders standard, highly functional layout presets.
- **No Telemetry Validation**: Local validation occurs strictly on-device. An upgrade check must never broadcast or query external usage trends without direct consent.
- **Zero Loss of User-Authored Data**: If a trial expires, the application gracefully transitions to the standard Free tier. **NO** user tasks, notes, or logs are ever wiped, hidden, or deleted.
